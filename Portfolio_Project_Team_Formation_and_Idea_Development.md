## 3. Decision and Refinement

### ✔ Selected MVP: Smart Inventory Tracker

A web-based platform engineered for small businesses to track real-time stock levels, automate low-inventory alerts, and manage orders efficiently.

### Why This MVP Was Selected
* Achieved the highest score across feasibility, team alignment, and real-world utility.
* Offers a clear division of tasks perfectly suited for a 4-person team (Auth, Database APIs, Dashboard UI, Notifications/Analytics).
* Demonstrates high value for showcasing full-stack proficiency in our engineering portfolios.

### Problem It Solves
Small retailers often struggle with manual tracking methods, which leads to:
* Out-of-stock events and lost sales.
* Human errors in ordering and stock counts.
* Lack of real-time visibility into inventory performance.

### Target Audience
* Small-to-medium retail businesses.
* E-commerce store managers.
* Warehouse and inventory coordinators.

### Key Features
* Secure User Authentication & Role-Based Access Control (Admin / Staff).
* Full CRUD operations for Products, Categories, and Suppliers.
* Automated Email & In-Dashboard Low-Stock Alerts.
* RESTful API architecture for potential third-party integrations.
* Analytics Dashboard showcasing stock velocity and sales trends.
