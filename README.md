# Maksab (مَكْسَب)

Maksab is a digital platform for Saudi home-based and family-production
businesses. It brings training, wholesale supplies, pricing tools, and trusted
business-service partners together in one place.

## Project Goals

- Help home-business owners calculate fair and profitable product prices.
- Make training, supplies, and business-support services easier to find.
- Support the growth and formalization of small and family-run businesses.

## MVP Features

- Smart pricing calculator
- Training academy
- Wholesale marketplace with a shopping cart
- Trusted partner directory
